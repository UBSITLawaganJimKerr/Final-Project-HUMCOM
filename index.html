GET TO KNOW US

Making mental health resources easier to find.

Enlighten is a student-designed platform that aims to make mental health information more accessible. It brings together local and online services, counseling information, support communities, and educational resources.

Our goal is to help people discover reliable information and take the first step toward finding appropriate support.

Our Mission

To create a welcoming and accessible space where everyone can discover mental health resources without feeling lost or overwhelmed.
